PlankPath CAD: Support

Updated October 4, 2026

Problems or ideas: beamtoolengineer@gmail.com. In the app, Help > Send feedback on Windows, or the feedback link in Help, prepares a draft with your app version and operating system.

Send a Windows log

The feedback draft names the log folder: %USERPROFILE%/PlankPath/logs. Open that folder in File Explorer, inspect the relevant log, and attach it yourself if it helps explain the problem. Include what you did and what you expected. Do not send passwords or private files you do not intend to share.

Restore Purchases

The iPad app is free and has nothing to restore. On Windows, use the same Microsoft Store account that made the purchase and restart the app to check your purchases again. When the store cannot be reached, the app keeps the last access it confirmed.

Cancel a subscription

The iPad app is free, so there is no subscription to cancel on iPad.

For a Microsoft-billed subscription on Windows, follow Microsoft's cancellation instructions. The store manages billing and cancellation.
