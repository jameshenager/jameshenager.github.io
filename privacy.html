PlankPath CAD: Privacy Policy

Updated October 4, 2026

PlankPath CAD has no app accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not upload your designs, shop information or logs to us.

Your files

Designs and shop settings stay on your device or in the location you choose, such as your PC or iCloud Drive. The browser keeps its local settings on that device. Your chosen file or cloud provider handles its own storage and synchronization.

Feedback and logs

Feedback is an email draft that you choose to edit and send to beamtoolengineer@gmail.com. No attachment is added automatically. Windows diagnostic logs stay on your PC unless you choose to share them. Review a log before attaching it: it can contain file paths, design names and error details.

Purchases and links

Apple or Microsoft handles purchases and payment details. The app asks its store for purchase entitlement and keeps the last confirmed access on your device. We do not receive your card details. Opening an external link or a public design address contacts the provider you chose; that provider's privacy practices apply.

Contact

For questions about this policy or an email you sent us, contact beamtoolengineer@gmail.com.
